GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

How do I request an estimate?

Call or text Oscar at (512) 758-0983. Include your property address and a description of the work. Photos can help explain the size and condition of your yard.

How much will my lawn service cost?

Pricing depends on the yard size, condition, and services requested. Contact Oscar for an estimate for your property.

Can I ask about recurring mowing?

Yes. Ask Oscar about current availability and a schedule that works for your lawn.

What if my project is not listed?

Describe what you need or send photos. Oscar can confirm whether the project is a fit and discuss next steps.
